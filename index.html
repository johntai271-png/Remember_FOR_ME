⚙️ BYOD Tablet Kiosk Setup Manual
1
Position Tablet Home Station: Place your legacy Android or iOS tablet on a sturdy stand in the living room, connected constantly to the wall charger.
2
Lock Screen/Kiosk Enforcement:

📱 iOS (iPads): Enable Guided Access via Settings > Accessibility. Open this browser app and triple-click the Home/Power button to lock the patient inside the screen.

🤖 Android: Enable Screen Pinning via Settings > Security. Open this browser app, view recent apps list, tap the app icon and select "Pin App".

3
Proximity Placement: Put the Bluetooth Smart Tag inside the patient's wallet or pocket, and fit the Wearable Safety Tracker on their wrist. The systems will sync automatically.
